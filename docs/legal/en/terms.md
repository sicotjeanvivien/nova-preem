## The service

Nova Preem lets you browse the card catalog, record the printings you own, track your completion, export and import your collection, and sync it with Google Drive if you want. It is published by AWSD (see the [legal notice](/mentions-legales)) and available at {{domain}}, in a browser or installed as an app.
