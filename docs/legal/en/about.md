# About Nova Preem

Your card collection, printing by printing.

Nova Preem is a free app to manage your Cyberpunk TCG card collection. It runs in your browser, installs on a phone or a computer, and keeps working without a network.

## Why

The same card comes in several printings: Retail, Beta, French, English, starters, demo decks, promos. Existing tools are mostly built for players and their decks. Nova Preem is built for collectors: knowing exactly which printings you own, which ones you are missing, and how far along each release is.

The idea comes from UrzaGatherer, the go-to app for Magic: The Gathering collectors.

## What the app does

- Browse sets, their releases and all their cards, in French and in English.
- Record what you own, printing by printing, with − and +.
- Track completion for each release and each set, and spot missing cards and duplicates.
- Search for a card across all sets and all languages.
- Export and import your collection, and sync it between your devices with Google Drive if you want.

## Your data stays yours

- No account, no ads, no analytics.
- Your collection is saved on your device. If you turn on sync, it is saved in **your** Google Drive, in a file you can access. We never see it.
- Details are in the [privacy policy](/confidentialite).
