# Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

## In short

- No account, no analytics, no ads, no cookies.
- Your collection is saved on your device. We have no server that receives it and we never have access to it.
- If you turn on sync, your collection is saved in your own Google Drive. It goes straight from your device to Google, without going through us.
- The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which sees your IP address, like any host.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is AWSD, SASU, 60 rue François Ier, 75008 Paris, France (see the [legal notice](/mentions-legales)). AWSD has not appointed a data protection officer. For any question: {{contactEmail}}.

## What stays on your device

The app stores in your browser (IndexedDB and the app cache):

- a copy of the card catalog and its images, to work offline;
- your collection: the printings you own, their quantities and the dates they were added;
- your settings;
- if sync is turned on: the email address of the connected Google account (to show it in Settings) and the ID of the sync file in your Drive.

This data leaves your device only if you export a file or turn on sync. To erase it, clear the site's data in your browser settings, or uninstall the app. Note: without a backup or sync, your collection is then lost.

## When you use the site

The site is hosted by Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages). To serve pages and protect the site, Cloudflare receives your requests, including your IP address and technical information about your browser. This data may be processed outside the European Union, in particular in the United States; Cloudflare participates in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. We have not turned on any analytics tool and we do not use these logs to identify visitors.

The catalog, images and fonts are served by the site itself: using Nova Preem sends no request to any third-party service.

## Google Drive sync (optional) {#google-drive}

If you turn on sync in Settings:

- **Sign-in**: you sign in to Google in a Google window. Google's sign-in library is loaded from `accounts.google.com`, only when sync is used.
- **Permissions requested**:
  - `drive.file`: create and edit **only** the files created by Nova Preem. The app has no access to the rest of your Drive;
  - `openid` and `email`: know the connected account's address, to show it in Settings.
- **What is saved in your Drive**: a `nova-preem-collection.json` file, in a "Nova Preem" folder, containing your collection and some of your settings (default release and language, completion, duplicate threshold).
- **What we receive**: nothing. Exchanges happen directly between your browser and Google. AWSD receives neither your sign-in token, nor your email address, nor your collection.
- Data obtained from Google is used only for sync. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising or to train artificial intelligence models. Nova Preem's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
- **Stopping**: Settings → "Sign out" stops sync on the device; the file stays in your Drive. To remove Nova Preem's access to your account: [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). To delete your data, delete the file in your Drive.

Google processes this data under its own rules: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Exported files

Exporting creates a file (JSON or CSV) on your device. You alone decide what to do with it.

## When you write to us

If you email us, your message and address are kept as long as needed to answer and follow up on your request. Our email is handled by Google.

## Links to other sites

Nova Preem links to other sites (cyberpunktcg.com, GitHub, Google Drive). They have their own privacy rules.

## Cookies

Nova Preem sets no cookies. The local storage described above is strictly necessary for the app you are using: it does not require consent.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, object, restrict and port your data. Most of your data is not with us: you exercise these rights directly, by exporting your collection (portability), editing it, or deleting it from your device and your Drive. For anything else, write to {{contactEmail}}. You can also file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority: [cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr).

## Changes

This policy may change, for example if card prices or new services are added. It will then be updated before the change, and the date at the top of the page changed.
